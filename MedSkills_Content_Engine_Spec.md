## 7. SCRIPT SKELETON (short-form, 60–90 sec / LinkedIn post)

1. **HOOK (line 1):** specific number OR sharp reframe. No throat-clearing. Never "Today I want to talk about…"
2. **TENSION:** name the exact pain of the tagged ICP. Make them feel seen, not pitied.
3. **REFRAME / VALUE:** the insight or category education they didn't have. This is the teach.
4. **PROOF:** one real data point, role, or named company. (No fabricated outcomes — see §9.)
5. **CTA:** route to the masterclass (intensity per campaign phase — see calendar). Plus one engagement question.

Formatting: short lines, white space, scannable. Max 3–5 hashtags. Hinglish allowed (see §10).

---

## 8. CTA STANDARD (every campaign post points at 4 July)

- **The honest framing (our differentiator):** no hype, no fake placement screenshots. E.g. *"Free masterclass on 4 July — the real MedTech roles that hire from a pharma background, and the honest difficulty of each. Register: {{REGISTRATION_LINK}}"*
- **Soft CTA (early phase):** "We're going deep on this in a free masterclass on 4 July. Link in comments."
- **Hard CTA (final week):** "Registrations close soon. [N] spots in the live room so we can actually answer questions. Register: {{REGISTRATION_LINK}}"
- Always pair the CTA with a self-identification question to drive comments (reach).

---

## 9. COMPLIANCE (hard rules — never break)

- **No fabricated placement numbers, salary claims, or student outcomes** unless from verified, consented cohort data. Until then: "stories like these" and ranges.
- **No fake scarcity.** If you state a seat cap, it must be real.
- Salary/market figures must be sourced (e.g. IBEF) and framed as ranges.
- Authority comes from Gagan's real 25-yr track record and real industry data — never invented results. Inventing them breaks the exact trust our best posts earn.

## 10. LANGUAGE & FORMAT

- **Hinglish:** English for technical credibility (MedTech, regulatory, clinical, device companies). Hindi/Hinglish for emotion and "you know this feeling" moments. Don't force it.
- Short paragraphs, heavy white space, one idea per line in hooks.
- 3–5 relevant hashtags max.
- Never the banned patterns in §5.
