# Engine 1 — LinkedIn Growth Engine (Build-Out)

*Part of the MedSkills Content OS (`MedSkills_Content_OS.md`). Version 1.0 · 6 July 2026. This is the operational engine: pillars, templates, frameworks, workflow, and SOPs. It produces content — it is not itself a filled calendar. Writing calibration lives in `MedSkills_Content_Engine_Spec.md` (canonical); this engine tells you what to make, when, in what mix, and how to run it.*

---

## 1. PURPOSE & PLACE IN THE FUNNEL

LinkedIn is the **top of the funnel** (Awareness Layer). Its only jobs:
1. **Grow reach** — primarily by activating Gagan's ~20.8k profile as the distribution node.
2. **Drive webinar registrations** (the monthly bridge object).
3. **Warm people into the WhatsApp community.**

It **never** cold-sells the 6-week Accelerator. The Accelerator appears only as "what comes after," inside the promotional 10%. Everything routes: **LinkedIn → webinar / community**, not LinkedIn → sale.

## 3. THE FOUR CONTENT PILLARS

Every post belongs to exactly one pillar. Monthly mix follows the OS ratio: **Map 40% · Insider 25% · Mirror 25% · Proof 10%** (Proof scales up as verified data arrives).

### Pillar 1 — THE MAP (Educational · 40%)
- **Job:** make MedTech careers *exist* in the reader's mind. The core top-of-funnel task.
- **ICP:** BOTH (skews ICP-1). **Voice:** Company Page (lead), Shilpi (soft entry).
- **Hook types:** category-education, role-decode, list/teardown.
- **Formats:** carousels (save-bait), numbered text posts.
- **Examples of angle:** "The 7 MedTech roles that hire from a pharma background," "5 skills that get an MR hired into a device company," one role decoded per post.

### Pillar 2 — THE INSIDER (Authority · 25%)
- **Job:** reach + credibility via Gagan's real 25 years.
- **ICP:** ICP-2. **Voice:** Gagan.
- **Hook types:** stat-threat, insider-truth ("what a hiring manager actually sees"), contrarian.
- **Formats:** first-person text posts, occasional data graphic.
- **Angle:** "I read a few thousand pharma CVs from the hiring side. Most got rejected in under a minute. Here's why."

### Pillar 3 — THE MIRROR (Community · 25%)
- **Job:** make the reader feel seen; harvest self-identification (the warm list).
- **ICP:** BOTH. **Voice:** Shilpi (lead), Company Page.
- **Hook types:** reframe ("it's the system, not you"), segment-the-reader.
- **Formats:** story-led text, "which one are you?" segment posts, polls.
- **Angle:** "Are you the MR who's hit the ceiling? The fresh grad watching peers get corporate jobs? Tell me below."

### Pillar 4 — THE PROOF (Trust · 10%, scales later)
- **Job:** show it's real and repeatable — compliance-gated.
- **ICP:** BOTH. **Voice:** Gagan / Company Page.
- **Hook types:** transformation story (documentary arc), webinar moments.
- **Formats:** story posts, webinar clips, quote graphics.
- **Compliance:** only real, consented stories (e.g. the webinar's Novartis→Boston Scientific arc with Deepankar's consent). No fabricated placements/salaries. Until cohort data is verified: "stories like these" + ranges.

## 4. POST-TYPE TEMPLATE LIBRARY (reusable skeletons)

Each template = the structure to fill via the generation spec. All follow the 5-part skeleton (hook → tension → reframe → proof → CTA) from `MedSkills_Content_Engine_Spec.md §7`. Fill, don't free-write.

**T1 · Role-Decode Carousel** (Map) — 7 slides + caption.
`Slide 1: Role name + "the door nobody showed you" hook · S2: what it actually is · S3: who hires (real companies) · S4: day-to-day · S5: honest entry difficulty · S6: where your background fits · S7: "full breakdown in the masterclass" + CTA.` Caption = 5-part skeleton, self-ID question.

**T2 · Stat-Threat** (Insider) — text.
`Line 1: hard specific sourced number → threat named bluntly → "if you think this won't reach you…" → one concrete proof (named companies) → soft CTA + self-ID question.`

**T3 · Reframe** (Mirror) — text.
`Persona can't get outcome → not a competence gap, a positioning gap → the reframe → your background is the head start → CTA.`

**T4 · Segment-the-Reader** (Mirror) — text, comment magnet.
`"Where are you right now?" → 3 "Are you the [persona] who…?" paragraphs (one per ICP variant) → "tell me which below" → soft CTA.`

**T5 · Founder Story** (Insider/Proof) — Gagan, text.
`Personal origin + constraint → the wall (systemic) → what he saw from inside → why he built MedSkills → soft invite.` The moat; zero compliance risk.

**T6 · Insider Truth** (Insider) — Gagan, text.
`"Here's what actually happens on the hiring side…" → the hidden mechanic → the fixable gap → "I teach this in the masterclass" → CTA.`

**T7 · List / Teardown** (Map) — text or carousel.
`"N things that [outcome]" → numbered, skimmable, one real proof point → "save this" + CTA.`

**T8 · Proof / Transformation** (Proof) — story, consent-gated.
`Real person + origin constraint → the wall → the switch + cost → the concrete outcome → "this is repeatable."` **Gate: verified consent required.**

**T9 · Webinar-Clip Post** (Map/Proof) — native video + caption.
`Hook from the clip's best line → 1-line context → "full session/replay" → CTA to register/community.`

## 5. WEEKLY & MONTHLY FRAMEWORK (repeatable template)

### Weekly grid (default 5 posts + weekend amplify)
| Day | Surface | Pillar | Template options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Company | Map | T1, T7 |
| Tue | Gagan | Insider | T2, T6 |
| Wed | Company/Shilpi | Mirror | T3, T4 |
| Thu | Gagan or Shilpi | Insider/Proof | T5, T8 |
| Fri | Company | Map / CTA | T1, T9 (webinar runway during webinar weeks) |
| Sat–Sun | All | Amplify | reshare with a personal take — no new post |

**Gagan carries ≥2 original posts/week** (the reach lever). The page amplifies Gagan, never the reverse.

### Monthly framework (20 slots, template — fill per month's theme)
- **Theme:** = the month's webinar topic (so LinkedIn pre-sells the webinar).
- **Slot allocation:** 8 Map · 5 Insider · 5 Mirror · 2 Proof = 20.
- **Rhythm:** Weeks 1–2 educate/warm; Week 3 webinar runway (tease → value → segment → founder invite → agenda → honest scarcity → last-call); Week 4 webinar + repurposed clips + recap.
- **Non-webinar months** (if any): replace runway with a mini-series or a "30-day knowledge sprint" and route CTAs to the community.

*The filled monthly calendar is the OUTPUT of running this framework — produced in the next step, not baked into the engine.*

## 6. PUBLISHING CADENCE & GAGAN AMPLIFICATION PROTOCOL

- **Cadence:** 5 original posts/week, consistent times, so the audience learns the rhythm. No bunching, no silence gaps (a June-audit failure to avoid).
- **Amplification protocol:** every strong Company/Shilpi post → Gagan reshares within 24h with a 1–2 line personal take (not a bare repost). Every Gagan post → Company page reshares. This is how a small page borrows a 20k audience.
- **Link discipline (2026 reach reality):** keep registration/community links **in the first comment**, not the post body; use **comment-a-keyword → DM** mechanics on launch-day posts to protect reach.

## 7. CTA LIBRARY

**By campaign phase (webinar runway):**
- **Soft:** "We go deep on this in a free masterclass on [date]. Link in comments."
- **Medium:** "Full breakdown + live Q&A in the masterclass on [date]. Register — link in comments."
- **Hard (real caps only):** "Registration closes when we go live. [N] live seats so Gagan can actually answer questions. Register: link in comments."

**Off-campaign (route to community):**
- "For weekly breakdowns like this, join the free MedSkills WhatsApp community — link in comments."

**Always pair the CTA with a self-identification question** (drives comments → reach). **Never** a cold Accelerator pitch here.

**Compliance:** any seat cap stated must be literally true; any stat sourced + ranged. See §10.

## 8. THE GENERATION WORKFLOW (how to actually produce a post)

1. **Pick the slot** from the monthly framework → gives you pillar, voice, ICP, template, CTA phase.
2. **Feed the generation spec** (`MedSkills_Content_Engine_Spec.md`): "Write [template] for [voice], [ICP], phase [x]" — or generate 3 options and choose.
3. **Engine returns tagged draft(s):** `[Voice · ICP · Hook type · Phase]` + the proven pattern it echoes.
4. **Compliance + quality gate** (§10) — auto-refuse fabrication/fake-scarcity/banned templates; rewrite, don't flag.
5. **Voice approval:** founder signs off on their own voice (Gagan/Shilpi posts).
6. **Design** if carousel (§ brand law).
7. **Schedule** to cadence; link in first comment; set amplification reshare.
8. **Log** in the tracking sheet for Engine 6.

---

## 9. ENGAGEMENT PLAYBOOK

- **First 60 minutes** after posting = the algorithm window: founder/page replies to every early comment (dwell + reach signal).
- **Reply protocol:** answer the actual question, add one more useful thing, invite a next step. On segment-the-reader posts, acknowledge each self-identification personally.
- **Harvest:** tag self-identifying commenters (by ICP) as warm leads → route to community / webinar. This is the warm-list machine.
- **Never** argue in comments; handle pushback with platform-data framing, per house style.

---

## 10. COMPLIANCE & QUALITY GATE (run on every post)

- [ ] Line 1 is a specific number OR a sharp reframe (no throat-clearing).
- [ ] Named ICP + real proof (companies/roles), no fabricated placements/salaries/outcomes.
- [ ] Any stat is sourced and framed as a range; any seat cap is literally true.
- [ ] Not a banned template ("[skill] is important ✅✅✅", emoji-bullet wall, adjective-dump).
- [ ] ≤ 5 hashtags. Hinglish only where it serves emotion.
- [ ] CTA matches campaign phase; self-ID question present.
- [ ] Correct voice; founder-voice posts approved by the founder.
- [ ] Link in first comment (not body).

## 11. MONTHLY SOP (one page)

- **Inputs:** month theme + webinar date/topic (from community-poll signal), the pillar map, the template library.
- **Outputs:** 20 scheduled, tagged, compliance-passed posts + weekend amplify plan + engagement + a filled tracking log.
- **Owner:** Content Lead (plan + draft + schedule + log); Founders (voice + approval + amplification + first-hour engagement); Designer (carousels).
- **Frequency:** monthly plan (Week 1) → weekly batch → daily publish + engage.
- **Tools:** generation spec, scheduler, Canva (brand kit), KPI tracking sheet, LinkedIn analytics.
- **Success criteria:** cadence hit (5/wk), pillar mix within ±1 slot of target, Gagan ≥2/wk, registration + community-join targets hit, ≥1 winning post/week identified for templating.
- **Weekly checklist:** [ ] batch drafted [ ] gate passed [ ] scheduled [ ] links in comments [ ] amplification set [ ] prior week's winners/losers logged.
