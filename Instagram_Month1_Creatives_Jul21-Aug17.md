# Instagram Month 1 - Creative Specs, 21 Jul → 17 Aug

Brand base is identical to the LinkedIn creative spec (BRAND.md): navy #0A2A43 / blue #00589E / cyan accent rationed, Fraunces headlines + Jakarta everything else, 60-30-10, mentor tone. IG-specific rules:

- **Reels:** 1080×1920, 25 to 60s. Hook text on screen inside second 1 (Jakarta Bold, white on navy strip). Burned-in captions always (most watch muted). Reel COVER is designed, not a frame grab: navy or canvas card, Fraunces hook line, face visible. Covers are what make the grid look like one brand.
- **Carousels:** 1080×1350, same skeleton as the LinkedIn/existing decks (navy title → light content → navy CTA). Last slide always repeats the comment keyword.
- **Grid discipline:** alternate navy-cover reels and light carousels so the profile reads as a designed grid. Logo small, quiet field only.
- **Stories:** 1080×1920, brand cards + raw phone footage mixed. Countdown stickers, question stickers, link stickers. Stories can be rougher than feed; that is the point.
- **Audio:** trending audio only at low volume under voice, never replacing it. Voice is the asset.

Videos marked (reuse) are the SAME files shot for the LinkedIn sprint on the Sun 20 Jul shoot; they were filmed vertical for exactly this. Only the end card changes: LinkedIn says "link below", IG says "comment APPLY".
