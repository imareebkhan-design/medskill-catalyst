Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

MedSkills Catalyst Pvt. Ltd. provides career-transition training and mentoring as a fully online service. We do not sell or ship any physical products. This policy explains how our programme and services are delivered after enrolment.

Digital delivery only

All MedSkills Catalyst programmes, masterclasses, and counselling sessions are delivered 100% online. There is no physical shipment, courier, or delivery of goods. Accordingly, no shipping charges apply to any purchase.

How and when you receive access

  • Enrolment confirmation: On successful payment, you will receive a confirmation email and WhatsApp message within 24–48 hours.
  • Onboarding: Our team will share your onboarding details, joining instructions, and cohort schedule via email and WhatsApp.
  • Programme access: Live sessions are conducted over Zoom, and course materials are shared through digital channels. Access to live cohort sessions begins on the official cohort start date published at the time of enrolment.
  • Counselling & masterclass sessions: Joining links are sent to your registered email and WhatsApp number ahead of the scheduled session.

Contact details you provide

Because delivery is fully digital, please ensure the email address and WhatsApp/mobile number you provide at enrolment are accurate. We are not responsible for missed access caused by incorrect contact details. If you do not receive your confirmation within 48 hours of payment, email billing@medskillscatalyst.com and we will resolve it promptly.

Questions

For any delivery or access issue, contact info@medskillscatalyst.com or billing@medskillscatalyst.com.
