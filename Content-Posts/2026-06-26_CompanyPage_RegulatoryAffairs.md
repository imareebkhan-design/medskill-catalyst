Regulatory affairs.

What it actually is

You're the person who gets a device cleared and keeps it compliant. You know the rulebook, the submissions, the standards a product has to meet before it can be sold and used. When the rules change, the company looks to you.

Who hires for it

Every device company has a regulatory team. Medtronic, Abbott, BD, Johnson & Johnson MedTech, Boston Scientific, plus the consultancies and CROs that handle submissions for smaller firms. Demand is steady, because nothing ships without this function signing off.

The day to day

Documentation, submissions, audits, and staying ahead of changing standards. Less time in hospitals, more time with files, teams and authorities. Detail is the job. If you were the MR who actually read the full prescribing information, this might suit you better than you think.

Honest difficulty: medium to hard

The rulebook is dense and the learning curve is real. But once you've climbed it, you're hard to replace, and the role travels across every company and product category.
