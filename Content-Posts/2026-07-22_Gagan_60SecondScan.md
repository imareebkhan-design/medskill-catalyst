## POST

When a CV opens on my screen, I've usually decided within a minute whether to keep reading. Every hiring manager works like this, whether they admit it or not.

Here's what I'm actually looking for in that minute.

First, can this person operate in a clinical setting. Not "have they sold a device," but do they understand the room, the clinician, and how decisions get made inside a hospital.

Then, have they shown they can learn a product and drive its adoption. Pharma people almost always have this. They just describe it as "target achievement" instead of what it really was.

Last, did they write the CV for this role, or send the same generic one they send everywhere.

That's the whole scan. It isn't a high bar, but most pharma CVs miss it because nobody told them what the reader is looking for.
