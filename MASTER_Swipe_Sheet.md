## PART 1 — THE 9 HOOK TYPES (swipe by pattern)

### A. STAT-THREAT HOOK — *lead with a hard number + name the denial*
**Formula:** `[Scary specific statistic]. → [The threat, named bluntly]. → "If you think this won't happen to you, you're already behind." → one concrete proof → soft CTA.`
**Why it works:** a number is undeniable; naming the reader's denial breaks scroll-past. This produced the single highest-engagement post in the entire dataset.
**Posts:** `#1` GrowthSchool (2,638 reactions ⭐ the outlier) · `3.1` Sisinty MBA-ROI (Batch1) · `3.3` Anshuman "80% of engineers unemployable" (Batch1)
**MedSkills adaptation (ICP-1):** "Lakhs of B.Pharm graduates pass out every year. Most end up as field MRs with a salary ceiling they hit by year 5. The corporate MedTech roles that pay 2–3x? Nobody tells you they exist." Lead with the number, name the trap, one proof, Gagan's face.

---

### B. CATEGORY-EDUCATION — *"what if the best career isn't the one you were shown?"*
**Formula:** `Most people think [field] = only [3 obvious paths]. → That's what [person] thought too. → But there's a hidden track: [unexpected specialisation]. → Here's the career it built. → "The ones who grow fastest step outside the traditional track."`
**Why it works:** this IS your top-of-funnel job — making the audience aware non-obvious paths exist. LawSikho runs this better than anyone.
**Posts:** `#18` LawSikho "most exciting legal career isn't a courtroom" ⭐⭐ · `IN.7` Bhaskar "How to get started in RA" · `IN.6` Bhaskar "3 skills for RA" · `2.4` GrowthSchool "38,000 startups need PMMs"
**MedSkills adaptation (ICP-1):** "Most pharma grads think the field is only: MR, retail pharmacy, or lab. That's what [name] thought too. Then she found regulatory affairs / medical affairs / market access — and here's the corporate career it built." Direct lift of `#18`'s skeleton.

---

### C. REFRAME — *"your situation isn't a liability — it's your advantage"*
**Formula:** `[Persona] can't get [outcome]. → That's not a [competence] gap, it's a [positioning/messaging] gap. → You're presenting your value in a way [gatekeepers] overlook. → Here's the reframe. → Your background is the head start.`
**Why it works:** directly answers ICP-2's core objection ("is my experience wasted? am I too late?"). The most transferable hook you have.
**Posts:** `1.6` Jebb Nurse→sales ⭐ · `1.7` Jebb Teacher→pharma · `1.15` Jacob "No experience, no problem" ⭐ · `1.19` Bagga "luck vs strategy" · `IN.3` Arun "the real MBA you earn on the road" ⭐ · `IN.2` Arun perseverance/33-yr-MR ⭐⭐ · `#16` LawSikho "most lawyers don't struggle because they lack knowledge" ⭐⭐ · `1.1`/`1.4` Pharma Coach
**MedSkills adaptation (ICP-2):** "Pharma MR for 6 years and can't get a corporate MedTech callback? That's not an experience gap — it's a positioning gap. Your territory, KOL access, and product knowledge are exactly what device companies hire for. You're just describing it in MR language, not MedTech language."

---

### D. CONTRARIAN / HARSH TRUTH — *say the uncomfortable thing the industry won't*
**Formula:** `[Widely-held belief] is wrong. → Here's what's actually true. → [Evidence/reasoning]. → What this means for you.`
**Why it works:** signals insider credibility; the cynical ICP-2 trusts someone willing to break ranks.
**Posts:** `3.1` Sisinty "degrees < proof of work" · `3.6` Anshuman "skill trumps everything" ⭐ · `3.4` Anshuman "credentials vs skills" · `IN.5` Arun "launches fail in the field, not the boardroom" ⭐ · `1.8` Jebb "recruiters aren't here to help you" · `IN.1` Arun "stop pushing product"
**MedSkills adaptation (ICP-2):** "Your years on the field aren't the problem. The problem is the industry sold you a ceiling and called it a career. Same product knowledge, same hospital access — different company, 3x the salary."

---

### E. TRANSFORMATION STORY — *the documentary arc (your highest-converting long-form)*
**Formula:** `Meet [real person + specific origin/constraint]. → The wall they hit (systemic, not personal). → The decision to switch + what it cost (hours, doubt). → The concrete outcome (role, % hike). → "This is repeatable."`
**Why it works:** dependable 60–160 reactions every time; it's your build-in-public + student-transformation bucket combined.
**Posts:** `2.6` Masai "Rahul — A FIGHTER" ⭐ · `#7` Scaler Shama (career gap) ⭐ · `#12` Scaler Mahendra (farm→SDE, 200% hike) ⭐ · `#17` Newton "gold medal but no career" ⭐ · `#11` Scaler learner voice · `#13` GS Siddharth · `1.13`/`1.20` Jacob/Chase · `IN.9` Abhineet destination role · `2.1` Scaler Rajandeep
**⚠️ COMPLIANCE:** every one of these names a real person + real numbers. Per your brand brief, only do this with **verified consent + cohort data**. Until then: "stories like these" / ranges, not named placements.
**MedSkills adaptation:** lift `2.6`'s skeleton wholesale — ordinary protagonist, systemic failure, intervention, concrete outcome — swap engineering details for an MR→MedTech or B.Pharm→regulatory arc.

---

### F. LIST / TEARDOWN — *numbered, skimmable, save-bait*
**Formula:** `[N] [mistakes / skills / habits] that [decide outcome]. → punchy numbered items → "what would you add?"`
**Why it works:** high save/share rate, easy to localize, positions you as the person who knows the rules.
**Posts:** `1.12` Jacob "10 mistakes breaking in" ⭐ · `1.5` Pharma Coach "10 bad habits in medical sales" · `1.2` Jebb "5 steps" · `IN.6` Bhaskar "3 skills for RA" · `IN.4` Arun "3 books" · `IN.10` Arun "field operating rhythm"
**MedSkills adaptation:** "7 reasons your pharma resume gets auto-rejected for corporate MedTech roles" / "5 skills that get a Pharma MR hired into a device company."

---

### G. INSIDER TRUTH — *how the system actually works (from the gatekeeper's side)*
**Formula:** `Here's what [recruiters/companies/hiring managers] actually do that nobody tells you. → the mechanic → what to do about it.`
**Why it works:** gives the reader a feeling of secret access; pairs perfectly with your "insider speaking to a younger version of themselves" tone.
**Posts:** `1.8` Jebb "recruiters paid by the company, 7-second scan" · `1.14` Jacob "cost of turnover" · `1.16` Bagga "read the job description" · `3.4` Anshuman "why companies fall back on pedigree" · `IN.5` Arun "what really decides a launch"
**MedSkills adaptation:** "What a MedTech hiring manager actually sees when a Pharma MR's resume lands — and the 10 seconds that decide it."

---

### H. FOUNDER / PERSONAL STORY — *build-in-public, vulnerability, named stakes*
**Formula:** `Personal moment / milestone → honest belief or struggle → what it taught me → a question back to the reader.`
**Why it works:** your differentiation lane. Brand pages can't fake this; founder honesty is your moat (and your brief's core strategy).
**Posts:** `3.5` Sisinty "8L education loan" ⭐ · `3.10` Prateek "Happy Birthday Masai / our beliefs" ⭐ · `IN.2` Arun perseverance ⭐⭐ · `IN.4` Arun "three books" · `#8` LawSikho founder "girl from Asansol" · `1.10` Dr Akram founder story
**MedSkills adaptation (Gagan):** "We don't have fake placement screenshots. Here's what we actually have after [X] months building MedSkills, and here's what we got wrong." Honesty IS the strategy.

---

### I. SEGMENT-THE-READER — *the comment magnet*
**Formula:** `"Where are you right now?" → 3–4 vivid "Are you the [persona] who…?" paragraphs, each naming a specific pain → then the offer/answer → ask them to self-identify in comments.`
**Why it works:** readers comment to say "this is me" → highest comment-to-reaction ratio in the dataset (LawSikho: 51 comments on 25 reactions).
**Posts:** `#15` LawSikho "Where are you in your legal journey?" ⭐
**MedSkills adaptation:** "Are you the MR who's hit the territory ceiling? The B.Pharm grad watching peers get 'corporate' jobs? The MBA from a non-tier-1 college with no clear healthcare path? Tell me which one below."

---

### J. PROOF / PLACEMENT — *receipts (use with compliance care)*
**Formula:** `[Outcome metric] → named people / partners → congratulations.`
**Posts:** `3.2` Prateek "20 offers, 14.37 LPA avg" · `#5` Codebasics placements roundup · `2.7` Masai press feature · `IN.8` Bhaskar placement proof · `3.9` Prateek thank-you note
**⚠️ Same compliance flag as E.** This is the bucket you must be most careful with until you have verified cohort data.
