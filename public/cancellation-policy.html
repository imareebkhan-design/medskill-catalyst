Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

This policy explains how to cancel your enrolment in a MedSkills Catalyst programme and what happens to your fees when you do. It works together with our Refund Policy.

How to request a cancellation

To cancel your enrolment, send a written request from your registered email address to billing@medskillscatalyst.com, including your full name, registered mobile number, and cohort details. Cancellation requests are only accepted in writing by email.

Cancellation within the 7-day window

If you cancel within 7 calendar days from the official cohort start date, you are eligible for a 100% refund of paid tuition fees under our 7-Day Fit Guarantee. Your request must reach us before 11:59 PM IST on the 7th day of the programme.

Cancellation after the 7-day window

Because we cap each cohort at 20 seats to protect the quality of mentoring and placement support, a late withdrawal takes a seat another candidate could have used. Therefore, cancellations requested after the first 7 days are non-refundable, and no partial or full refund will be issued for voluntary withdrawal, non-completion, or a change in personal circumstances.

EMI and financing plans

If you enrolled using a third-party financing or No-Cost EMI option (for example through HDFC, ICICI, Axis, or Bajaj Finserv):

  • Cancelling within the 7-day window releases you from the financing obligation, and we will coordinate with the financier to cancel the loan agreement.
  • After the 7-day window, you remain legally obligated to service the EMI payments directly to the financing institution per your loan terms.

Prefer to defer instead of cancel?

If a medical or professional emergency prevents you from continuing, you may request a one-time deferral to the next scheduled cohort instead of cancelling. Deferral requests must be made in writing and are subject to verification and approval by the admissions committee.

Refund processing

Where a refund is due, it will be processed to your original payment method within 7–10 business days of approval. Timelines for the amount to reflect in your account depend on your bank or card issuer.

This Cancellation Policy is a starting template provided for convenience and is not legal advice. Please have it reviewed by qualified counsel and tailored to your business before you publish.
