## POST

If you studied biomedical engineering, biotech, or life sciences, this one is for you.

Most of the career talk you hear is built around pharma. B.Pharm, M.Pharm, medical rep, and so on. So it's easy to feel like MedTech conversations aren't really about you.

They are. A lot of MedTech was built around exactly your background.

Biomedical engineers understand the devices themselves. Biotech and life-science graduates understand the science the products are based on. Clinical application roles, product support, quality, and regulatory all lean on people who can speak both the technical and the clinical side at once. That is you.

The reason it doesn't feel that way is simple. Nobody sat you down and connected your degree to these jobs. Woh gap aapki galti nahi hai.
