## What the engagement data actually tells you (read this before you copy anything)

**1. The 2,638-reaction outlier is the single most important data point here.** Every other post on every page sits under ~250 reactions. GrowthSchool's viral post won by ~10x — and it's worth dissecting *why*:
- **A hard, specific, scary statistic as the first line** ("1.5M finish engineering, only 6% placed") — not a feeling, a number.
- **Names the threat directly** ("even coding is being taken over by AI") and **calls out the reader's denial** ("if you're thinking 'this won't happen to me' you're already behind").
- **Concrete proof** (the founder's own AI sales workflow: 2 hours → 10 minutes).
- It rode a **creator collaboration** (Raj Shamani's "Figuring Out") — distribution, not just copy.
- **Your version:** "X lakh pharma/life-science graduates enter the market every year. Most end up as field MRs with a salary ceiling by year 5. And the corporate MedTech roles that pay 2–3x? Nobody tells you they exist." Lead with the number, name the trap, show one concrete proof, ride a credible face (Gagan).

**2. Transformation stories are the reliable workhorses** (Shama, Mahendra, Nandita, the LawSikho learners). They cluster at 60–160 reactions consistently. The winning structure is identical every time: **ordinary person + real constraint (farm family / career gap / gold medal but no job) → systemic problem → the program → concrete outcome (200% hike, Senior BA, SDE).** This is your documentary-script skeleton. Compliance note: every one of these names a real person and often real numbers — only do that with verified consent + cohort data, exactly as your brand brief requires.

**3. LawSikho is your closest playbook, even at lower raw engagement.** It's the only page running true **category-education** ("what if the most exciting career isn't a courtroom?") and **reframe hooks** ("most lawyers don't struggle because they lack knowledge"). Swap "lawyer/courtroom" for "pharma grad/medical rep" and you have ready MedSkills scripts. Its 51-comment poll-style post also shows the **"segment the reader" device** (4 "Are you the one who…?" paragraphs) is a comment magnet — useful when you want reach via comments.

**4. What underperforms:** pure announcements (awards, hackathon recaps), culture clips, and feature-dump hiring posts get reactions but little that's transferable. Don't model these — they work off existing audience size, not craft.

**Bottom line:** you now have 68 posts across all files. The highest-signal templates for MedSkills are (a) GrowthSchool's stat-led threat hook, (b) the universal transformation arc, and (c) LawSikho's category-education reframe. If you want, next I'll merge all files into one master swipe sheet tagged by hook-type and ICP — that's what turns this pile into something your writers can actually pull from.
