## POST

Most pharma-to-MedTech applications get rejected for one avoidable reason, and it has nothing to do with talent.

The resume.

It's not a formatting problem, it's a translation problem. People list what they did in pharma terms and assume the hiring manager will connect the dots to a device role. They usually won't, because there are forty other CVs in the pile.

A few changes that quietly make the difference:

Lead with the clinical relationships and settings you've worked in, not the drug names.
Show adoption and outcomes, not only target percentages.
Use the words the job description uses. If it says "clinical stakeholders," don't write "doctors I visited."

None of this is invention. It's presenting real experience in the language the reader recognises.
