# Prompt for Claude Design — MedSkills Catalyst Advanced Module (Overview Brochure, No Pricing)

Copy everything below the line and paste into Claude Design.

---

**Create a premium, print-ready A4 PDF brochure (4 pages, portrait, 210×297mm) introducing the MedSkills Catalyst Advanced Module — the Career Roadmap Program.** This is an *overview* brochure: it explains what the programme is, who it's for, and what it changes. It must contain **no pricing, no package tiers, and no add-on price list.** The commercial conversation happens after the discovery call.

Apply our existing brand system — colours, typography, logo placement, and spacing rules — throughout. Do not introduce off-brand colours or fonts.

## Audience & tone
MedTech professionals in India — clinical application specialists, sales and product specialists, marketing/regulatory/quality professionals, engineers and pharma professionals moving into MedTech, and emerging leaders. Tone: confident, credible, consultative. Think premium advisory firm, not a coaching bootcamp. Avoid hype language, exclamation marks, and stock "success" clichés.

## Design direction
- Generous white space. Because there's no pricing table, the layout carries the weight — let it breathe.
- Strong typographic hierarchy: large display headings, clear section eyebrows, 10–11pt body text at 1.5 line height.
- Brand accent colour used sparingly, as a highlight — on stage numbers, key phrases, and the closing CTA only.
- Thin-stroke geometric line icons in a single accent colour. No illustrations or heavy photography. If one photograph is used, keep it to the cover with a brand-colour overlay.
- Subtle full-bleed colour blocks to separate sections. No gradients heavier than a 10% tint.
- Quiet footer on every page: logo mark, page number, website.
- Print-safe: CMYK-friendly colours, 12mm minimum margins, nothing critical within 5mm of the trim edge.

## Page-by-page structure

**Page 1 — Cover**
- Logo, top left.
- Eyebrow: *Advanced Module*
- Headline: *Career Roadmap Program*
- Sub-headline: *Personalised Career Advisory for MedTech Professionals*
- Hero line, set large and bold: *Don't just change jobs. Build the right MedTech career.*
- Footer strip: website and contact email.

**Page 2 — Why This Program + Who It's For**
- Opening statement: Many MedTech professionals feel stuck despite strong technical capability. This programme provides personalised guidance, strategic mentoring, and a practical roadmap for career growth.
- Two-column split: left = the problem (3 short pain-point statements, muted grey), right = the answer (3 short outcome statements, brand accent).
- Below, an icon grid of the five audience segments:
  Clinical Application Specialists · Sales & Product Specialists · Marketing, Regulatory & Quality Professionals · Engineers & Pharma professionals transitioning to MedTech · Emerging and aspiring leaders

**Page 3 — The Career Journey**
Render as a five-stage roadmap with numbered nodes and a connecting line. Each stage gets a title and one supporting line:
1. **Career Discovery** — Where you are, what you're built for, and where the market is going.
2. **Gap Analysis** — The specific skills, exposure, and credentials standing between you and the next role.
3. **Personal Brand** — Resume, LinkedIn, and positioning that make recruiters come to you.
4. **Career Acceleration** — Interview readiness, salary strategy, and a 90-day execution plan.
5. **Career Partnership** — Ongoing mentorship and access to the alumni network.

**Page 4 — What You Walk Away With + Call to Action**
- Top half: a clean 3×3 card grid of the nine deliverables, each with an icon and label. Present these as *outcomes*, not as a package inventory — no quantities, no tier labels:
  Personalised Career Strategy Report · Skill Gap Assessment · 90-Day Development Plan · ATS Resume · LinkedIn Optimisation · Mock Interview Feedback · Salary Negotiation Strategy · Career Action Plan · Alumni Community Access
- Middle: a **Before / After** comparison block, side by side with a visual divider.
  - Before: Unclear direction. Limited visibility. Slow growth.
  - After: Clear roadmap. Stronger personal brand. Promotion readiness. Recruiter visibility. Long-term mentorship.
  - "Before" in muted grey, "After" in brand accent, so the contrast reads instantly.
- Close with a full-width brand-colour CTA band. Because there is no pricing in this brochure, the CTA must do the selling:
  - Headline: *Find out which track fits you.*
  - Line: *Book a free 15-minute discovery call. We'll map where you are, where you want to be, and what it takes to get there.*
  - Contact email (info@medskillscatalyst.com), website, and space for a QR code linking to the booking page.
  - Optional single line, small: *Programme tracks and investment details shared on the call.*

Export as a high-resolution, print-ready PDF with selectable text and clickable links. Also provide a screen-optimised version under 5MB for WhatsApp and email sharing.

## Notes for you (not part of the prompt)

Because pricing is removed, the CTA is now the single most important element in the document — the brochure's only job is to get someone onto a discovery call. Make sure the booking link and QR code are live before you print or send this.

Two things worth adding if you have them:
- **One testimonial** on page 2 or 4, with name, role, and company. Without pricing to anchor value, social proof is what makes the reader act.
- **Programme format** — one line on duration and whether it's 1:1 or group. Readers who can't find this often assume it's a generic course.
