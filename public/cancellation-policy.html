Refund, Cancellation & Deferment Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Refund Policy

At MedSkills Catalyst, every admission is processed after careful review and seat allocation. As a result, all program fees paid are non-refundable.

Any request for a refund will be considered solely at the discretion of the Management based on the individual merits of the case. Submission of a refund request does not guarantee approval.

Refunds, if approved, shall be determined entirely by the Management after reviewing all relevant facts and supporting documentation.

2. Cancellation Policy

Once admission has been confirmed, cancellation requests may be submitted in writing.

However, cancellation of enrollment does not automatically entitle the student to any refund. Every cancellation request will be reviewed by the Management on the merits of the case, and the final decision shall remain binding.

3. Deferment / Postponement Policy

Students who are unable to continue their enrolled cohort due to unforeseen circumstances may request a one-time deferment to a future cohort.

Approval of any deferment request shall be entirely at the discretion of the Management after evaluating the circumstances and supporting documents submitted by the student.

Approval of a deferment is not guaranteed.

4. Medical Emergencies & Exceptional Circumstances

In cases involving genuine medical emergencies or other exceptional situations, students may submit a written request along with relevant supporting documentation.

Based on the nature of the case and supporting evidence, the Management may, at its sole discretion:

  • Approve or reject a refund request.
  • Approve or reject a cancellation request.
  • Approve or reject a deferment to a future cohort.
  • Provide any other resolution deemed appropriate.

The decision of the Management shall be final and binding.

5. Contact for Requests

All refund, cancellation, and deferment requests must be submitted in writing via email.
