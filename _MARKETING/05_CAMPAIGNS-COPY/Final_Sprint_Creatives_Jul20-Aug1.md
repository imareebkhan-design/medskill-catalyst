# Final Sprint - Creative Specs, 20 Jul → 1 Aug

Every creative follows BRAND.md. Non-negotiables repeated here once so each spec stays short:

- **Colors:** canvas #F7F9FB / white dominant (60%), navy #0A2A43 + blue #00589E structure (30%), cyan #4AD0FF accent only, rationed (10%). Never cyan text on light. Body ink #0F1B27, captions slate #5A6B7B.
- **Type:** Fraunces 500/600 for headlines only. Plus Jakarta Sans for everything else. Labels: Jakarta 600, UPPERCASE, wide tracking.
- **Layout:** generous whitespace, hairline borders rgba(10,42,67,0.08), 20/28px radii, soft navy-tinted shadows. No gradients on UI, no neon, no emoji walls, no black shadows.
- **Carousel base:** 1080×1350 px (4:5), matches the existing MedTech-Map/Roles carousel system: navy title slide → light content slides → navy CTA slide. Logo small, bottom corner, quiet field only. Page dots + "MedSkills Catalyst" footer as in existing decks.
- **Video base:** vertical 1080×1920 for repurposing (LinkedIn accepts it; also becomes the IG reel). Phone + lapel mic, plain light wall or navy backdrop, natural light. Burned-in captions: Jakarta SemiBold, white on navy strip, lower third. Hook text on screen in first 2 seconds. No background music louder than voice.
- **Tone:** mentor, not marketer. Numbers and named companies over adjectives. Nothing that couldn't be said to a student's face.

## Wed 22 Jul - The 60-second scan · VIDEO (Gagan, 60-75s talking head)

On-screen hook (0-2s): "Your CV gets 60 seconds. Here's where they go."
Script beats (Gagan, direct to camera, unscripted feel, one take):
1. "I read CVs for 25 years at companies like Pfizer and Medtronic. When yours opened on my screen, you had about a minute. Here is exactly how it got spent."
2. "First ten seconds: current company and role. I am placing you in the market."
3. "Next fifteen: therapy area, and who you actually sat across from. GPs or surgeons. That decides which role you map to."
4. "Then I look for one line that proves you ran something. A territory. A launch. Not an achievement wall."
5. "The last seconds go to the gaps. Hops without a story. Buzzwords. That objective statement about being a dynamic team player? Never once helped a candidate."
6. "Notice what I never got to. Your college. Your percentage."
7. CTA: "We fix exactly this in Cohort 1. Applications close 31 July. Link's below."
Edit notes: cut on every beat, caption strip throughout, b-roll optional (cursor scrolling a blurred CV mock, 2 inserts max). End card: navy, Fraunces "60 seconds. Spend them right." + logo.
