## 4. THE GAPS (in priority order)

### GAP 1 — There is no masterclass funnel. At all. *(critical, blocks the entire goal)*
Not one post mentions a masterclass. No registration link, no date, no offer, no lead capture, no "join the list." If the goal is masterclass registrations, **the content currently has nowhere to send anyone.** Every other gap is secondary to this one. You're building good top-of-funnel with no funnel attached.

### GAP 2 — Gagan's voice and reach are invisible. *(highest-leverage fix)*
Every post is faceless company-page voice. Meanwhile your co-founder has **25 years across Pfizer, BMS, and Medtronic** and roughly **100x the company page's reach** on his personal profile. The single biggest lever you have is moving founder-voice content to Gagan's profile and amplifying from the company page. A 53-reaction post on 194 followers becomes a very different number on 20k.

### GAP 3 — No human beings. No founder story, no student story, no faces.
Pure concept posts. Your brief says the student/founder is the hero and honesty is the strategy — yet there's no protagonist anywhere. Even compliance-safe options (Gagan's own Pfizer→Medtronic→"why I built this" arc; "stories like these" framing) are unused.

### GAP 4 — ICP-2 is addressed intellectually, never emotionally.
The 26.9% post speaks *to* the stuck MR with data, but nothing yet hits the gut fears your brief names ("is it too late?", "does my experience even count?"). The segment-the-reader comment magnet (#15) and the "it's the system, not you" reframe (#16) are missing entirely.

### GAP 5 — Generic "skill is important" template is dead weight.
Posts 4–8 follow one formula: "[Skill] is important. ✅✅✅ Here's a list. The future belongs to professionals who…" No number, no story, no named ICP, no tension — the exact anti-patterns the swipe file flags. They earned 5–13 reactions and they make the page feel like a content mill. Retire this template.

### GAP 6 — No lead capture / waitlist object.
Even before a masterclass exists, there's no email or WhatsApp capture. Per the GTM model, the masterclass registration IS your bridge between awareness and the selective cohort — without a capture mechanism, warm audience evaporates.

### GAP 7 — Cadence and formatting are inconsistent.
Posts bunch up (multiple at "3w") then go quiet. Emoji-bullet walls (✅🔹📱) on the old posts vs clean line-spacing on the new ones. No predictable rhythm an audience can follow.

## 5. THE CONTINUATION PLAN (oriented to masterclass registration)

This plugs directly into the two-layer GTM model we built. **The masterclass is the bridge object** — the concrete event that turns awareness-layer content into selective-cohort leads. Everything below is sequenced toward filling that masterclass.

### Step 0 — Define the masterclass first (this week, before any more posting)
You cannot drive registrations to something that doesn't exist. Decide:
- **Topic:** Make it the payoff the page already promised — *"The 7 MedTech roles that hire from a pharma background — and the honest entry difficulty of each."* Post 1 literally pre-sold this. Don't invent a new hook; finish the one that's working.
- **Format:** Free, live, 60–90 min, Gagan on camera (his 25 years IS the draw).
- **Date + registration page + lead capture** (email/WhatsApp). This is the missing funnel.
- **The honest CTA** (your GrowthX-inverted line): *"Free masterclass. We'll show you the real map and the honest difficulty of each door — no hype, no fake placement screenshots."*

### Step 1 — Turn the live series INTO the registration runway (next 2–3 weeks)
You already started a series. Re-aim it so each post ends by routing to the masterclass:
- Keep the proven formats (stat-threat, reframe, category-education).
- Each "role" post = a teaser; the full breakdown + Q&A = "in the free masterclass on [date]. Register here."
- Add the two missing emotional hooks: **segment-the-reader** ("Are you the MR who's hit the ceiling? The fresh grad watching peers get corporate jobs? Tell me below — and come to the masterclass") and the **"it's the system, not you" reframe.**

### Step 2 — Activate Gagan (the highest-leverage move, start immediately)
- Gagan posts **founder-voice** versions on his personal profile: his own Pfizer→BMS→Medtronic arc, "why I built MedSkills," the honest state of the company. (Format H/I from the GTM doc — zero compliance risk.)
- He personally invites his network to the masterclass.
- Company page amplifies/reshares. **This is how a 194-follower page borrows a 20k audience.**

### Step 3 — Registration-window cadence (the 10–14 days before the masterclass)
A predictable drumbeat, every post carrying the register CTA:
1. Tease (the promise) → 2. Value post (one role, specific) → 3. Segment-the-reader → 4. Founder invite (Gagan, personal) → 5. "Here's exactly what you'll learn" → 6. Honest scarcity ("we cap the live room so we can actually answer questions") → 7. Last-call.

### Step 4 — Convert masterclass → selective cohort (ties to the GTM doc)
Masterclass attendees = your warm list. The masterclass ends with the selective-cohort invitation ("we interview before we admit — because we won't take money from people we can't place"). The masterclass is the awareness→selective bridge made concrete.
