## CAROUSEL SLIDES (7)

**Slide 1 — Cover**
The MedTech role most pharma people never apply for.
Mostly because nobody told them they qualify.
Product Management.

**Slide 2**
What it is: you own a device's whole story in the market. What gets built, how it's positioned, how it's sold. Part strategy, part clinical, part commercial.

**Slide 3**
Who hires: Medtronic, Abbott, Boston Scientific, Stryker, Siemens Healthineers, Philips, GE Healthcare, and plenty of mid-size firms you've never heard of.

**Slide 4**
Day to day: you work with R&D, clinical and sales teams, study the competition, plan launches, and sit with clinicians to see how the device actually gets used.

**Slide 5**
Why a pharma background fits: you already read clinicians and hospitals well, and you know how a product gets adopted. That instinct is most of the job.

**Slide 6**
Honest entry difficulty: harder to enter cold than sales. Most people move in from device sales, clinical, or marketing first. Step one is knowing it exists.

## CAPTION

Ask a pharmacy or life-science grad to name a MedTech job and you'll usually hear "sales." Fair enough, it's the one everyone sees.

Product management is the one they miss.

It rewards exactly what a pharma background gives you. You understand clinicians, you understand hospitals, and you have a feel for how a product gets adopted on the ground. Nobody tells you that counts for anything.

The companies hiring for it are names you already know: Medtronic, Abbott, Boston Scientific, Siemens Healthineers, Philips, GE Healthcare.
