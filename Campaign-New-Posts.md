## Jul 29 (Wed) — What applying actually looks like · Company · Hard · [HOLD: confirm steps, enroll link, close date]

A few of you keep asking what applying actually involves, so here is exactly what happens after you click the link.

First, you fill a short form. Where you are now, your background, what you are trying to move into. It takes about ten minutes and there is no payment at this stage.

Then we read every application ourselves. This is the reason we cap the cohort. If it looks like a fit, we set up a short conversation, usually fifteen to twenty minutes.

Then the interview. This is not a test you pass or fail on qualifications. It is us making sure you are serious and that we can genuinely help with where you want to go. It also gives you the chance to ask us anything before you commit a rupee.

If it is a match on both sides, you are in, and you get everything you need before the cohort starts on 1 August.

The reason we do it this way instead of a checkout button is simple. We are going to be in the room with you for six weeks, and both of us should be sure it is the right call before it starts.

Applications close 31 July. If you have been putting it off, starting the form tonight gives us time to actually talk before then.
