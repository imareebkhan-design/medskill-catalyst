Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

MedSkills Catalyst Pvt. Ltd. ("MedSkills Catalyst", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use medskillscatalyst.com or register for our masterclass, counselling calls, or programmes, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a form on this site, we collect the details you provide: your name, WhatsApp number, email address, and current professional background. We also collect basic analytics data (such as pages viewed and approximate location) through cookies and tools like Google Analytics to understand and improve the site.

How we use your information

  • To contact you on WhatsApp, phone, or email about the masterclass, counselling sessions, and programme admissions you have expressed interest in.
  • To send programme updates, schedules, and joining links.
  • To improve our website, content, and services.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example, scheduling, messaging, payment, and analytics providers), and only as needed to deliver our services, or where required by law.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide our services and for legitimate business or legal purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or stop contacting you, at any time by emailing info@medskillscatalyst.com. You can opt out of WhatsApp updates by replying STOP.

Cookies

This site uses cookies for analytics and to remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some site features may not function as intended if you do.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@medskillscatalyst.com.
