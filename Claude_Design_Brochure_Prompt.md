**Create a premium, print-ready A4 PDF brochure (6 pages, portrait, 210×297mm) for the MedSkills Catalyst "Career Roadmap Program."** Apply our existing brand system — colours, typography, logo placement, and spacing rules — throughout. Do not introduce off-brand colours or fonts.

## Audience & tone
MedTech professionals in India — clinical application specialists, sales and product specialists, marketing/regulatory/quality professionals, engineers and pharma professionals moving into MedTech, and emerging leaders. Tone: confident, credible, consultative. Think premium advisory firm, not a coaching bootcamp. Clinical precision meets human warmth. Avoid hype language, exclamation marks, and stock "success" clichés.

## Design direction
- Generous white space. Let the layout breathe — this signals premium.
- Strong typographic hierarchy: large display headings, clear section eyebrows, comfortable 10–11pt body text with 1.5 line height.
- Use the brand accent colour sparingly, as a highlight — on numbers, stage markers, pricing tiers, and CTAs only.
- Use geometric line iconography (thin stroke, single accent colour) rather than illustrations or photography-heavy layouts. If photography is used, keep it to one clean hero image with a brand-colour overlay.
- Subtle full-bleed colour blocks to separate sections. No gradients heavier than a 10% tint.
- Every page must carry a quiet footer: logo mark, page number, and website.
- Print-safe: CMYK-friendly colours, 12mm minimum margins, no critical content within 5mm of the trim edge.

## Notes for you (not part of the prompt)

Fill these gaps before you send it, or the brochure will read thin next to competitors:

**Add if you have it:**
- **Programme format and duration** — how many weeks, 1:1 vs group, live vs recorded, hours per session. Buyers scan for this first and it's currently missing entirely.
- **Who delivers it** — mentor names, years in MedTech, companies they've worked at. In advisory services, credibility of the advisor *is* the product.
- **Proof** — 2–3 short testimonials with name, role, and company, or a stat ("83% of participants moved roles within 6 months"). One testimonial per page beats a whole testimonial page.
- **Company logos** where alumni now work, as a trust strip on page 2 or 6.
- **A clear next step** — booking link, WhatsApp number, or "Book a free 15-minute discovery call." Right now the brochure ends without telling anyone what to do.

**Worth considering:**
- **Cohort dates / limited seats** — creates urgency and justifies the premium pricing.
- **EMI or instalment options** on the ₹30k+ tiers. In the Indian market this materially lifts conversion at that price point.
- **A short FAQ** (4–5 questions) — "Do I need to be in MedTech already?", "What if I'm switching from pharma?", "Is there a refund policy?"
- **What's *not* included** — stating plainly that you don't guarantee placement builds more trust than staying vague about it.
- **A refund or satisfaction policy**, even a limited one, de-risks the Executive Advisory tier.
- The price *ranges* invite negotiation. Consider fixed prices on the brochure and handle variation in conversation.
