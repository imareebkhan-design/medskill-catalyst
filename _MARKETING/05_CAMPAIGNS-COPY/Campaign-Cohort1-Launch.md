# Cohort 1 Launch Campaign — 12 Jul → 1 Aug 2026

**One campaign, one story, one objective: move every reader one step closer to applying for the 6-Week Career Accelerator, Cohort 1 (starts 1 Aug).**

This is not a content calendar. There are no standalone posts. Each post inherits belief from the one before it and sets up the one after it. If a post doesn't move the audience along the belief ladder below, it doesn't ship.

- **Anchor decision:** Announcement lands **Mon 13 Jul** (per the "Week 2" tab runway, which also corrects 18 Jul = Saturday). This gives ~18 days of conversion runway into 1 Aug — the right length for an interview-gated founding cohort.
- **Compliance:** Scarcity must be literally true. All unconfirmed specifics are tokens (`{{SEATS}}`, `{{PRICE}}`, `{{ENROLLMENT_LINK}}`, `{{COMMUNITY_LINK}}`, `{{POLL_X}}`, curriculum modules). Posts touching those carry **[HOLD]** and do not publish until the real value replaces the token. No fabricated placements, salaries, or seat numbers.
- **Voices:** Gagan (authority / ICP-2), Shilpi (empathy / ICP-1), Company (hub). Gagan carries ≥2 posts/week.
- **Every post:** line-1 hook · named ICP · real proof · self-ID question · ≤5 hashtags · link in FIRST COMMENT · humanized (no em dashes, no forced triads).
