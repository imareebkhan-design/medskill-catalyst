# MedSkills Catalyst — LinkedIn Banner Design Prompts

Paste one of the **ready-to-use prompts** (Section B) into Claude / your design tool, and **attach the person's photo** when it asks. Section A is the reusable master template if you want to make more. Section C is how to shoot/prep the photo so the cut-out looks clean.

The style is modelled on the editorial reference you shared (big headline, photo bleeding in from the side, ↘ eyebrow, pill tags, soft gradient, faint oversized glyph), rendered in the MedSkills Catalyst brand system.

---

## A. MASTER TEMPLATE (reusable — fill the [BRACKETS])

```
Design a premium, editorial LinkedIn banner. Output a single flat image, [SIZE], no rounded corners on the canvas itself.

STYLE REFERENCE (composition):
- Large editorial headline occupying the right two-thirds, left-aligned within that block.
- A short eyebrow label in the TOP-LEFT, prefixed with a "↘" glyph, UPPERCASE, wide letter-spacing.
- The person's photo as a clean cut-out (no card, no box) bleeding in from the RIGHT edge, cropped so it sits behind/beside the headline. Cool-graded to match the palette.
- 2–3 rounded "pill" tags along the BOTTOM (outline pills, thin border), each prefixed with a small "↘", placed centre-right.
- One faint, oversized decorative glyph low-opacity in the background (an asterisk OR the MedSkills molecule/flask mark). Subtle, never loud.
- Generous whitespace. Calm. Lots of breathing room. Think high-end editorial, not a busy ad.

BRAND SYSTEM (strict):
- Background: LIGHT — soft gradient from canvas #F7F9FB to pale-blue #E8F2FB, with a gentle warm-to-cool light wash. (Dark variant: deep navy #0A2A43 field instead.)
- Headline font: Fraunces (serif), weight 600, tight negative tracking. Colour: navy #0A2A43 on light (white on navy variant).
- Eyebrow, pills, any labels: Plus Jakarta Sans, UPPERCASE for the eyebrow, tracking ~0.3em.
- ACCENT: exactly ONE keyword in the headline coloured primary blue #00589E. Optionally one inline "↗" arrow in blue.
- Cyan #4AD0FF may appear ONLY as a non-text decorative shape (e.g. the asterisk/glyph or a thin rule). NEVER as text on a light background (it fails contrast). Never put body text in blue or cyan.
- Feel: premium, calm, editorial, credible. NOT clinical, NOT corporate, NOT hype. No ECG/heartbeat lines, no neon glows, no drop shadows, no gradients on text.

CONTENT:
- Eyebrow: [EYEBROW]
- Headline: [HEADLINE]  (set the word "[ACCENT WORD]" in blue #00589E)
- Pills: [PILL 1] · [PILL 2] · [PILL 3]
- Photo: use the attached image of [NAME]; cut out the subject, place bleeding from the right.
- Place the MedSkills Catalyst logo (attached) small in the top-right OR as the faint background glyph — on a quiet field, never recoloured or distorted.

LAYOUT SAFETY:
- Size [SIZE].
- Keep the LOWER-LEFT corner clear: [SAFE ZONE] is reserved (LinkedIn overlays the profile photo / company logo there). Put nothing important in that corner.

INTEGRITY: invent no statistics, names, logos, or claims. Use only the text given above.
```

## C. PHOTO DIRECTION (so the cut-out looks premium)

For Gagan and Shilpi, send a photo that cuts out cleanly:
- **Shoulders-up or half-body**, looking at or near the camera, warm and composed (not a stiff corporate stare).
- **Plain or simple background** — easiest to cut out cleanly. A busy background makes the cut-out look cheap.
- **Even lighting**, no harsh shadows on the face.
- **Business-casual**; they should read as a senior, credible insider.
- Higher resolution is better. If the only good photo has a messy background, tell the design tool: *"cleanly remove the background and place on the brand gradient."*

Attach the photo **and** the logo file (`brand/logo/MedSkills-Catalyst_Logo.png`) with the prompt.

---

### One note on fonts
If your design tool can't load **Fraunces** or **Plus Jakarta Sans**, tell it to use the brand fallbacks: headings → *Cormorant Garamond* (or Georgia); body/labels → *Inter*. Don't let it default to a generic system serif.
