# MedSkills Catalyst — GTM Sequencing: The Selective-Cohort Model

*Adapting the GrowthX strategy (scarcity + curation as a moat) to MedSkills' two-ICP, compliance-bound reality.*
*Built on: the 68-post swipe file, the Batch 1 + Batch 2 competitor reports, and your brand brief. Date: June 2026.*

---

## 0. THE ONE IDEA THIS WHOLE DOC HANGS ON

GrowthX gates a club of people who've **already arrived**. MedSkills serves people who feel **stuck and gatekept**. So you cannot copy GrowthX's *reason* for selectivity (status flex) — you must invert it:

> **GrowthX:** "We only let impressive people in." (exclusivity as a flex)
> **MedSkills:** "We interview before we admit because we refuse to take money from people we can't actually place. We are not the EdTech scam you're afraid of." (selectivity as a trust + outcome-protection signal)

Same mechanic — a gate. Opposite emotional payload. The MedSkills version is also the *honest* version, because you genuinely are a small founding team that can only run small, high-touch cohorts. **Your scarcity is real, not manufactured — that truth is the entire marketing asset.** The moment you fake the number of seats, you've committed the same sin as faking a placement stat.

---

## 1. THE TWO-LAYER ARCHITECTURE (the spine of everything below)

Scarcity and reach fight each other. You resolve that by running two layers with different jobs, and never letting them bleed.

| | **Awareness Layer** | **Selective Layer** |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Job** | Reach + trust + category education | Convert the warmest into a paying cohort + protect outcomes |
| **Energy** | Open, mass, welcoming, Hinglish, founder-honest | Gated, scarce, high-touch, standards-driven |
| **Model reference** | Bada Business accessibility + Steve Adamson advocacy | GrowthX selectivity |
| **Channel** | Gagan's LinkedIn + YouTube, public | Application → interview → cohort |
| **What it must NEVER do** | Sound exclusive or salesy (kills reach) | Open the gates to everyone (kills the signal) |
| **Primary metric** | Reach, self-identification comments, waitlist signups | Application→admit rate, cohort fill, then verified placement |

**The bridge between them is a single object: the waitlist / application.** Awareness content drives people to apply. The application's selectivity is then broadcast *back* into the awareness layer as proof of standards. That loop is the flywheel — Section 3.

## 2. THE SEQUENCE (phased, with what to post and why)

The competitor report's 4-stage arc — Credibility → Community → Proof → Authority — still holds. The selective overlay changes *when the gate appears* and *how the gate becomes content.* Timing assumes you start from ~20k on Gagan's profile and zero verified cohort data.

### Phase 0 — Foundation (Weeks 0–2, no public posting yet)
Decide and document three things before a single selective post goes out:
1. **Cohort size** — pick a genuinely small, honest number you can deliver high-touch (e.g. 15–25). This number must be true.
2. **Admission criteria** — what makes someone a good-fit admit vs a "not yet." Write it down. (This is what you'll later show publicly as proof of standards.)
3. **The reject pathway** — what a "no" comes with. Every rejection must hand the person a next step (a free resource, "reapply after you do X"). A bad "no" creates an enemy and feeds the cynicism; a good "no" creates a future applicant and an advocate.

### Phase 1 — Credibility + Category (Months 1–3, AWARENESS LAYER ONLY)
No selling. No gate yet. You are earning the right to be selective later — **a velvet rope in front of an empty room is just arrogance.**
- **Gagan founder-honesty posts** (Format H / swipe #8, IN.2): "Here's what we actually have after X months building MedSkills, and what we got wrong." Honesty IS the strategy.
- **Category-education posts** (Format B / swipe #18, the "multiple doors" Format G): "Most pharma grads think the field is MR, retail, or lab. There are at least 4 corporate doors you've never seen." This is the top-of-funnel job — make people aware the careers exist.
- **Steve Adamson "advocate, not salesman" POV posts** (Format I): small, real, encouraging, no link. The zero-risk trust builder. Sprinkle between structured posts so the feed never feels like a campaign.
- **Open a WAITLIST, not a sale.** The waitlist is your first scarcity signal and your warm-list capture. "We're opening a small first cohort soon. No pitch yet — join the list if you want to know when."

### Phase 2 — Community + Self-Identification (Months 2–4, overlaps Phase 1)
Convert passive followers into a warm, self-identified list.
- **Segment-the-reader posts** (Format I in swipe / #15, the 51-comment magnet): "Are you the MR who's hit the territory ceiling? The B.Pharm grad watching peers get corporate jobs? The non-tier-1 MBA with no healthcare path? Tell me which below." Self-identification = your warm-list, by ICP.
- **A "30-Day MedTech Knowledge Sprint"** (Format E / LawSikho challenge model): daily micro-lessons, people comment answers. Builds community + shows depth + generates cohort leads. This is your Medical-Sales-Mentors-cluster "high-frequency touch" lesson, run as a public campaign.
- **Application for Cohort 1 opens — small and selective.** Now the gate appears, only after the audience is warm.

### Phase 3 — Run the Selective Cohort + Make the Gate into Content (Months 3–5)
This is the GrowthX mechanic, converted into trust content.
- **The "why we interview" post** (Format J): "We turned down [N] applicants for this cohort. Not because they weren't good enough — because we couldn't honestly promise them an outcome yet. Here's what we told them to do first." This single narrative does more for the scam-cynic than any testimonial.
- Keep awareness-layer posting running in parallel. The two layers run simultaneously; they just speak differently.

### Phase 4 — Proof (Months 5–9, gated behind REAL data)
Only once Cohort 1 has **verified, consented outcomes.**
- **Kraftshala-style public placement reports** (Format B in competitor report): "Cohort 1: [X]% placed within [Y] weeks, roles, salary ranges, hiring companies." Even 5–10 real placements beat any "our alumni are thriving" line.
- **Until that data exists, you say "stories like these" and use ranges.** Non-negotiable per your brand brief.
- This is the moment the gate's value compounds: "Last cohort placed [X]. Next cohort has [N] seats." Proof + scarcity, both true.

### Phase 5 — The Flywheel (ongoing)
Each cohort's verified proof + genuine selectivity raises demand for the next gate; awareness content keeps the top of funnel full. Authority/contrarian posts (swipe #1, #16, IN.5) sustain reach and keep the algorithm warm.

---

## 3. HOW THE TWO LAYERS FEED EACH OTHER (the flywheel)

1. **Awareness content** plants "these careers exist" + "this founder is honest" → grows + warms the audience.
2. **Self-identification posts + Sprint** convert followers into a warm list, tagged by ICP.
3. **Application opens** for a genuinely small cohort → scarcity is real because the team is small.
4. **Selective admission** → admits feel *chosen* (the GrowthX effect); rejects get a kind path forward.
5. **The selection itself becomes awareness content** ("why we said no to [N] people") → trust signal → more applications next round.
6. **Verified cohort outcomes** → proof content → feeds back into awareness AND raises the value of the next gate.

The engine only spins if Phase 1 happens before Phase 3. Reach first, gate second.

## 4. ICP TAILORING (same architecture, two emotional entry points)

### ICP-2 — Stuck professionals (MR / lab / hospital, 25–38, cash + cynicism)
- **Lead emotion:** "Your experience is the head start, not the liability — and we screen precisely so we don't waste your money." The anti-scam reframe matters *most* here.
- **Hooks:** Reframe (#16, IN.2, IN.3), insider-truth (IN.5, 1.8), "treat the switch like a territory" (Format H — speaks their native target/territory language).
- **Why they're the first paying cohort:** highest intent, have the ₹20k+, and the trust reframe converts their cynicism into loyalty. Anchor Cohort 1 here.

### ICP-1 — Fresh graduates (B.Pharm/M.Pharm/BSc/MBA, 21–27, price-sensitive, confused)
- **Lead emotion:** "The map is bigger than you were shown — and we admit on potential and fit, not pedigree." (Directly answers the non-tier-1 grad's pedigree wound; echoes Anshuman's Scaler Fairplay logic, swipe 3.4.)
- **Hooks:** Category-education (#18, Format G), paradox (#17 "gold medal but no career"), stat-threat (#1) for reach.
- **The tension to manage:** ICP-1 is your bigger top-of-funnel but has less cash. Pure selective + expensive shuts them out. Give them a *pathway*, not just a rejection: a lower-tier entry, a scholarship-style seat, or "earn your way in via the Sprint." Selectivity for ICP-1 should feel like a ladder, not a wall.

## 5. STRESS-TESTS / WHERE THIS BREAKS (read before executing)

1. **Gating before you have an audience = velvet rope, empty room.** If you open the selective cohort in Month 1 with no warm list, scarcity reads as delusion. Phase 1 is not optional.
2. **Rejection done coldly damages the brand and feeds cynicism.** Every "no" ships with a path forward. Budget real effort here — it's not an afterthought, it's brand-defining.
3. **Fake scarcity = fake placements.** If you say 15 seats, take 15. Manufactured urgency is the exact "EdTech scam" energy your ICP-2 smells instantly. The whole play dies if the scarcity is a lie.
4. **Layer bleed kills reach.** The day your category-education posts start sounding exclusive or salesy, your top funnel collapses. Discipline: awareness = welcoming, selective = gated. Never blend.
5. **ICP-1 lockout.** If selective + premium becomes the only door, you forfeit your largest awareness audience. The ladder/scholarship pathway is what keeps ICP-1 in the system.
6. **You're not GrowthX-scale and shouldn't pretend to be.** GrowthX gates 4,000 members at Apple/Google. You're gating a 15-person founding cohort. Your power isn't prestige — it's *honesty + high-touch + a credible founder face.* Lean on that, not on borrowed exclusivity theatre.

## 6. WHAT TO MEASURE

- **Awareness layer:** reach, follower growth, profile views, **self-identification comments** ("this is me"), **waitlist signups.** (Comments-to-reactions ratio is your warmth signal — see #15's 51:25.)
- **Selective layer:** applications, application→interview rate, **admit rate** (keep it honestly selective), cohort fill, completion rate, then — only when verified — placement rate, roles, salary ranges.
- **Flywheel health (the real KPI):** does each cohort's published proof increase the *next* cohort's application volume? If yes, the engine is spinning. If no, the proof or the reach is too weak — fix upstream, don't push harder on the gate.

Join the list."* That one sentence is the entire GrowthX strategy, inverted for your audience, and it's 100% compliance-safe.
