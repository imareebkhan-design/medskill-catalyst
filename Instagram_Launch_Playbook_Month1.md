# Instagram Launch Playbook — Month 1 (21 Jul → 17 Aug)

*Objective: build Instagram into MedSkills Catalyst's second distribution engine. Support the Aug-1 Cohort-1 close in the first 10 days, then build the follower base that makes Cohort 2 dramatically cheaper to fill.*

---

## 0. FOUNDER-LEVEL HONESTY FIRST (read this before the tactics)

An account launched on 21 Jul with 0 followers will not fill Cohort 1. It CAN: (a) catch the Instagram-native slice of your warm LinkedIn/WhatsApp audience during urgency week, and (b) become the compounding asset for Cohort 2 (Sep/Oct). So Month 1 has two jobs, in order:

1. **Days 1–11 (21–31 Jul):** convert warmth you already own. Point every LinkedIn post, WhatsApp broadcast, email and webinar audience at the IG account. Urgency-week stories.
2. **Days 12–28 (1–17 Aug):** grow cold reach with reels + collabs + ambassadors, powered by real Cohort-1 footage (the proof engine LinkedIn can't match visually).

## 1. THE DISTRIBUTION PRINCIPLES (the Varun Mayya / Aeos lens, applied)

1. **Format-market fit beats content quality.** On Instagram India (530M+ users, largest market in the world), reels are the reach engine: the algorithm tests each reel on a small sample; seconds 1–2 decide everything. 70% reels / 30% carousels. Carousels are for saves (career content is save-heavy); reels are for reach.
2. **Faces win. Hinglish wins.** Bada Business proved founder-face + Hindi accessibility = mass reach in India (study the reach, reject the guru hype). Gagan and Shilpi ARE the channel. Company-page-style graphics without faces will die on IG.
3. **One shoot → ten assets.** Every Saturday: film 6–8 talking-head reels in one 2-hour session (phone + lapel mic + plain wall is enough). Every LinkedIn carousel → IG carousel (resize). Every webinar → 5 clips. Never create for IG from scratch.
4. **The DM is the funnel.** IG links are weak; DMs are strong. Every reel CTA = "comment [WORD] and I'll DM you the guide/link." Use ManyChat (or Manychat-style keyword automation) from day 1. Comments → DMs → WhatsApp community → application. This also multiplies reach (comments = ranking signal).
5. **Borrow audiences before you own one.** Collab posts (co-authored reels appear in both accounts' feeds), micro-influencer barter, and campus ambassadors are how a 0-follower account reaches thousands in week 1. Solo posting alone = months of silence.
6. **Volume with a floor, not perfection.** 1 reel/day is the goal; 4–5/week is the floor. Expect 0–500 followers in month 1, 1,000–2,000 by month 3 with consistency — unless one reel outliers, which in this niche (career anxiety + Hinglish) is genuinely likely. Play for the outlier by shipping volume.

## 6. INFLUENCER MARKETING — yes, but micro and barter-first

Skip celebrity/macro influencers (wrong audience economics, wrong trust profile for a "no-hype" brand). Target **micro-influencers (10k–100k) whose followers ARE your ICPs**:

- **Who:** pharma-career pages, B.Pharm/M.Pharm student creators, "medico motivation" pages, nursing/life-science career pages, GPAT/NIPER prep pages, medical-rep meme pages (surprisingly on-ICP for ICP-2).
- **Offer ladder:** (1) Barter: free masterclass seats + a giveaway for their audience, co-branded. (2) Collab post: they co-author a reel with Gagan ("I asked a guy who hired at Medtronic for 25 years what actually gets you in") — appears on both feeds, you gain their reach. (3) Paid shoutout/reel only for accounts that prove engagement (check comment quality, not follower count). Typical micro rates in India are modest — negotiate 3-post packages, never one-offs.
- **Vet ruthlessly:** engagement rate >3%, comments that sound human, audience geography India, no fake-follower spikes.
- **Target: 4 collabs in month 1** (2 barter, 1 collab-post, 1 paid test). Track follower delta + WAITLIST comments per collab.
- **Partner program (month 2+):** convert the best-performing influencers into standing affiliates — unique code, revenue share per enrolled student (disclosed). Only after month-1 data shows who actually converts.

## 7. AMBASSADOR PROGRAM — "MedSkills Campus Catalysts"

Your unfair advantage: ICP-1 sits concentrated in pharmacy/life-science colleges, and campus-ambassador culture in India is mature (students actively seek these roles on Internshala/LinkedIn for CV value).

- **Recruit 10–15 students** (B.Pharm/M.Pharm/biotech final-years) from your existing warm list first — webinar attendees who engaged most, then an open call post.
- **They get:** official certificate + LoR from Gagan (ex-Pfizer/Medtronic signature carries real weight), free masterclass + workshop access, a monthly mentorship group call with Gagan (this is the real currency), leaderboard prizes, and priority interview for future cohorts. Cash optional later; recognition + access + mentorship converts better than ₹500 stipends.
- **They do (keep it simple — 3 asks/week):** share 2 posts to their stories + WhatsApp college groups, gather 5 leads/week for the masterclass (unique referral link), and 1 monthly campus touchpoint (seminar-room session using your carousel deck).
- **Structure:** private WhatsApp group, weekly 15-min check-in, monthly leaderboard, 6-week terms (matches cohort rhythm; refresh each cohort).
- **Compliance guardrail:** ambassadors get a 5-line "what you may never claim" sheet (no placement promises, no salary claims). They amplify honest content only.
- **Why this cracks distribution:** 15 ambassadors × ~1,000 reachable classmates each = a 15,000-person warm network that no ad budget buys, refreshed every cohort, and it doubles as your Cohort-2 applicant pipeline.
