## POST

The most common thing we hear from people in our community: "I can't get into MedTech because I don't have device experience."

It sounds reasonable. It's also mostly wrong.

Almost nobody starts with device experience. Deepankar, who spoke at our masterclass, started as a Novartis medical rep. Most people now inside MedTech came from pharma, from clinical roles, or straight out of college.

What companies actually look for at the entry point is simpler than a device background. Do you understand the clinical setting, can you hold a real conversation with a doctor, and can you learn a product quickly. A pharma or science background already covers most of that.

So "no device experience" isn't the wall people think it is. The real gap is usually that nobody showed them how to present what they already have.
