Not sure how to break into the MedTech industry? In this MedTech 101 masterclass, industry leaders from Boston Scientific, Medtronic, Pfizer and Stryker explain how pharmacy, life-science and engineering graduates can build a career in medical devices: the roles that exist, the skills you need, and how people actually get hired.

Every year India produces thousands of talented pharma and life-science graduates, and most finish their degrees without ever hearing that these careers exist. This session fixes that. You'll hear from three people who have done it, including one who moved from being a Novartis medical rep to a leadership role at Boston Scientific, and one who has spent years on the other side of the table deciding who gets hired. There's also a live Q&A where they answer questions from the audience.

What you'll learn:
✅ Why so many pharma and life-science graduates never hear about MedTech careers
✅ How to move from pharma into MedTech, told through a real Novartis-to-Boston Scientific story
✅ The main career paths: sales, marketing, clinical, application and L&D
✅ The technical and business skills employers look for in freshers
✅ The resume mistake that quietly kills most fresher applications
✅ A practical roadmap for becoming industry-ready

Who this is for:
Pharmacy graduates (B.Pharm / M.Pharm), Life Sciences students, Biotechnology students, Biomedical Engineering students, B.Tech graduates, fresh graduates, early-career professionals, and anyone switching careers into MedTech.
